# Privacy and responsible use

Resume files, parsed contact data, qualifications, model inputs and analyses are personal data. Use only documents you are authorized to process. Applicant uploads stay private until explicit application consent grants a specific job's recruiter access. Private job analysis grants no sharing rights.

Withdrawal removes the application and associated shared analyses. Recruiter access through that job stops immediately. Another independently consented application can still permit access. Already downloaded documents cannot be technically recalled; recruiters must honor their retention obligations.

Resume/account deletion removes live database access and queues physical file deletion. A functioning dispatcher is necessary to drain this outbox. Backup deletion follows a separately configured retention schedule. Define and communicate retention before handling production personal data.

Do not collect protected attributes for ranking. Contact sections and recognized demographic lines are excluded, but unstructured language and educational/employment histories can encode proxies. The model has not been independently audited for hiring fairness. Scores describe documentary relevance, not inherent ability, job performance or hiring probability.

No automated rejection, identity inference, demographic prediction, or guaranteed hiring recommendation is provided. Unknown qualifications are labeled as uncertain/not documented. Human reviewers must inspect source evidence, corrections and extraction limitations.

Synthetic demo environments must stay isolated. Do not upload personal resumes to an open demo. No third-party LLM is called; downloads retrieve model software/weights, and inference executes locally in the worker.
